Privacy policy: Bogwobblers

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bogwobblers is a game for phones, made by Fair Weather Games, an independent game developer in the UK. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

What the game keeps

To remember your progress, Bogwobblers saves a few things on your phone:

  • your scores and best scores, and whether you've played today's Daily Bog
  • your streak, badges, the creatures you've found and the Hedgerow animals you've unlocked
  • your acorns
  • your sound and vibration settings
  • your "quirky stats", such as how often you've tapped and how long you've played

These stay in the game's own storage on your phone. The game never sends them to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, that backup may include them, as it would for any other app.

What the game doesn't do

Bogwobblers doesn't:

  • ask for your name, age, email address or any other details
  • have accounts, sign-ins or chat
  • use analytics or tracking of any kind
  • show adverts
  • sell anything inside the game
  • use your location, camera, microphone or contacts

It plays without an internet connection and doesn't send anything about you or your games over the internet.

Sharing a picture

If you tap Share at the end of a game, Bogwobblers draws a picture of your jar and your score and passes it to your phone's share menu. The picture is kept for a moment in the game's own storage on your phone so that your phone can share it. You choose where it goes. The game doesn't see or keep a record of where you send it.

Children

Bogwobblers is made for everyone, children included. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either. There are no adverts, no purchases, and no way to talk to other players.

Deleting your information

Everything the game keeps is on your phone. To delete it, uninstall Bogwobblers or clear its storage in your phone's settings. We don't hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Google Play

When you download Bogwobblers from Google Play, Google handles the download and anything it collects as part of that, under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bogwobblers changes any of this, for example by adding adverts, we'll update this page before that version comes out and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Bogwobblers: fairweathergames@protonmail.com

If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've answered a privacy question, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
