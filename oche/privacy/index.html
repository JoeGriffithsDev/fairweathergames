Privacy policy: Oche

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Oche is a darts game for phones, made by Fair Weather Games, an independent game developer in the UK. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

What the game keeps

To remember your games, Oche saves a few things on your phone:

  • the crawl you're in the middle of: which pub and round you're on, your score, your money, the regulars at your bar and the darts you've thrown this visit, so you can carry on where you left off
  • your records: how many crawls you've played and won, the furthest pub you've reached, your best visit, and which regulars you've met
  • how hard you usually flick (the strength of your last few throws), so the game can judge a flick against your own usual one
  • your sound, vibration and other settings, and whether you've seen the how-to-play cards and the first tips

These stay in the game's own storage on your phone. The game never sends them to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, that backup may include them, as it would for any other app.

What the game doesn't do

Oche doesn't:

  • ask for your name, age, email address or any other details
  • have accounts, sign-ins or chat
  • use analytics or tracking of any kind
  • show adverts
  • use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or motion sensors

It plays without an internet connection and doesn't send anything about you or your games over the internet. Your finger's movements on the screen are used on the spot to aim and throw, and never stored or sent anywhere.

Buying the full game

The version of Oche on Google Play doesn't sell anything at the moment. If a later version offers a one-off purchase to unlock the rest of the game, Google Play will handle the payment, and we'll update this page before that version comes out. We will never see your payment details.

Sharing your crawl

If you tap Share at the end of a crawl, Oche draws a postcard of how far you got, your best visit and the regulars at your bar, and passes it to your phone's share menu. The picture is kept for a moment in the game's own storage on your phone so that your phone can share it. You choose where it goes. The game doesn't see or keep a record of where you send it.

Who it's for

Oche is set in pubs and is made for teenagers and adults. It collects nothing from anyone, whatever their age, and has no way to talk to other players.

Deleting your information

Everything the game keeps is on your phone. To delete it, uninstall Oche or clear its storage in your phone's settings. We don't hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Google Play

When you download Oche from Google Play, Google handles the download and anything it collects as part of that, under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Oche changes any of this, for example by adding a purchase, we'll update this page before that version comes out and change the date at the top.
